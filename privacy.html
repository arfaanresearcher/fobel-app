Privacy Policy — Fobel

Fobel by INLABS · Last updated 9 August 2026

Summary

Fobel is a food & beverage costing tool for UAE hospitality teams. It is designed to be privacy-preserving: Fobel does not require an account and does not collect, store, or share personal information.

What data Fobel uses

  • Your inputs — recipes, prices, targets and vendor sheets you enter or import stay on your device (in the browser or app) and are not transmitted to us.
  • Market prices — the app downloads a public market-price reference file (UAE retail and Ministry of Economy benchmarks) to show comparisons. This is a one-way download of public data; nothing about you is sent with the request.

What Fobel does not do

  • No accounts, logins, or passwords.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs that profile you.
  • No collection of contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is no personal data to sell or share.

Data storage & deletion

Any data you enter lives locally on your device. Clearing the app's data (or uninstalling it) removes it. There is no server-side copy for us to delete.

Children

Fobel is a business tool intended for hospitality professionals and is not directed at children.
